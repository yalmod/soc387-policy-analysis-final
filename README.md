# SOC387 Policy Analysis Project: Compassionate Intervention Act in Alberta
Final policy analysis project for University of Toronto course SOC387: Family Policy. Completed December 2025.

Qualitative content analysis study.

Under the direction of Professor Joanna Pepin.

## Project Description
The project is a policy report on the Compassionate Intervention Act passed in Alberta on May 2025. Under this act, an authority figure may apply for the detention of a drug user, with or without their consent, to treatment. This policy is one attempt of many across Canada to address the national rise in drug toxicity deaths.

The report focuses on family policy and the impact on youths. I analyze the claims and sources of multiple points of view, present three policy options to address the issue, and recommend the policy most effective for all stakeholders.

Conclusions are made by analyzing government documents, reports, news media, opinions pieces, etc. Thorough source analysis may be found in the section "Source Evaluation Appendix".
